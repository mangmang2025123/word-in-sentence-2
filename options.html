Contextual Query Copier Options

To customize the hotkey (default is Ctrl+Q) for the "Copy word and context" action:

  1. Open Chrome's extensions page by navigating to chrome://extensions.
  2. Find the "Contextual Query Copier" extension in your list.
  3. Click on the "Details" button (or look for a menu icon if the layout has changed).
  4. Find and click on "Keyboard shortcuts" (or "Extension shortcuts"). This will take you to chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
  5. Locate the "Contextual Query Copier" section. You should see the "Copy word and context as a question" command.
  6. Click the edit icon (usually a pencil) next to the command and press your desired key combination.
